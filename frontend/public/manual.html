Ancentrix Voice Platform

Your complete guide to setting up and running AI-powered sales calls. From registration to campaign execution — everything you need in one place.

📋 Platform Overview

Ancentrix Voice is an AI-powered telephony platform that automates outbound sales calls. The AI agent pitches your products, handles objections, qualifies leads, and logs everything — so your human team can focus on closing hot leads.

🤖 AI Sales Agent

An intelligent voice bot that introduces itself, pitches products, answers FAQs, and qualifies leads in real-time.

📞 Telephony Integration

Connects via Vobiz for call routing. You get a dedicated phone number for your campaigns.

📊 Lead Management

Import leads in bulk (CSV/XLS), organize into batches, and track status from New → Warm → Hot.

📅 Smart Scheduling

Schedule AI call campaigns with start/end times. Calls fire automatically with 10-second gaps.

Dashboard URL: https://ancentrixhindi-livid.vercel.app/dashboard
Supported Languages: Hindi / Hinglish (with plans for more)
Call Type: Outbound only (Inbound not supported in current version)
Two separate platforms are involved in setup: Ancentrix Voice (this platform, where you configure your AI agent and run campaigns) and Vobiz (an independent third-party telephony provider, at console.vobiz.ai, that actually places the phone calls). Registration, KYC, and your phone number all happen on Vobiz's own site — Ancentrix never collects your Aadhaar/PAN or handles KYC directly. You just paste the three credentials Vobiz gives you into Ancentrix's Telephony settings. Steps 1 and 2 below walk through exactly which site to use for what.

1 Vobiz KYC & Ancentrix Signup

Do these in order — the Vobiz side is a prerequisite, because Ancentrix's Telephony settings need real credentials to save.

Part A — Vobiz Telephony Account (prerequisite)

🌐 Done on console.vobiz.ai
1

Register on the Vobiz Console

Vobiz is the independent telephony provider Ancentrix uses to actually place and receive calls. Go to https://console.vobiz.ai/ and create an account there — this is separate from your Ancentrix login.

2

Complete KYC Verification (on Vobiz, not Ancentrix)

KYC (Know Your Customer) is mandatory and is handled entirely by Vobiz. You will need to upload:

  • Aadhaar Card — for identity verification
  • PAN Card — for tax and business verification
Important: This KYC process happens entirely on the Vobiz console — Ancentrix does not collect or store these documents. Your Vobiz credentials and phone number only become available once Vobiz approves your KYC.
3

Note Your Credentials

Once approved, Vobiz's dashboard shows your auth_id, auth_secret, and assigned phone number. Keep this tab open — the next section shows exactly where to find them, and you'll paste them into Ancentrix shortly.

Part B — Ancentrix Account

🎙️ Done on the Ancentrix Voice platform
1

Register on Ancentrix

Go to the Ancentrix Voice platform and click Register. Fill in your basic details to create your account — this is where you'll configure your AI agent and run campaigns.

2

Choose a Plan

Select the subscription plan that suits your call volume. Plans vary by:

  • trial plan
  • Standard plan
  • Customized plan

Once your KYC is approved on Vobiz, log in to the Vobiz console (console.vobiz.ai) — not your Ancentrix dashboard — to view your credentials. This is a separate login from your Ancentrix account.

7 Prompts Configuration

Navigate to Settings → Prompts. This is the brain of your AI agent — it defines behavior, tone, and conversation rules.

Inbound Prompt: Not required in the current version. Leave it empty. Only outbound calls are supported.

Outbound System Prompt

This prompt tells the AI how to behave during sales calls. A well-crafted prompt dramatically improves call quality.

Recommended Optimized Prompt

You are a concise sales executive. Pitch only the assigned product using verified company and product facts. Understand the lead, answer the latest question first, handle objections naturally, and qualify interest without sounding like a form.

Never invent price, location, availability, address, features or appointment slots. If information is missing, say it cannot be confirmed and offer human follow-up.

Never book appointments. If the caller asks to end the call, say a brief goodbye and disconnect politely.

✅ Do's

  • Use verified facts only
  • Answer the most recent question first
  • Handle objections naturally
  • Qualify interest conversationally
  • Offer human follow-up when stuck
  • Be concise and professional

❌ Don'ts

  • Never invent prices or features
  • Never make up locations or addresses
  • Never invent availability or appointment slots
  • Never book appointments
  • Don't sound like a rigid form
  • Don't ignore the caller's request to end
Customize the prompt for your industry. For Real Estate, add: "If the lead asks for a site visit, say our team will coordinate and collect their preferred time."
Remember to click SAVE after configuring all Settings tabs. Unsaved changes will not take effect.

⭐ Best Practices

🎯 Before You Start

  • Complete KYC and verify your phone number is active before scheduling
  • Test with 5-10 leads first before launching a large batch
  • Ensure your product details and FAQs are accurate and up-to-date
  • Write a custom prompt tailored to your industry and product

💬 During Campaigns

  • Monitor Call Logs daily for the first week to catch any issues early
  • Review AI notes to identify common objections and update FAQs accordingly
  • Don't schedule overlapping batches — this can exceed your concurrent call limit
  • Keep fallback transfer number staffed during active campaigns

📈 Optimizing Performance

  • Update the "Active Product to Pitch" when running campaigns for different products
  • Refine your prompt based on actual call transcripts — add handling for new objections
  • Use lead status filters to prioritize hot leads for human follow-up
  • Track conversion rates by batch to identify best-performing audiences

🔒 Security & Compliance

  • Never share your auth_secret in emails or chat
  • Store credentials in environment variables, not in code
  • Ensure you have consent to call all numbers in your lead list
  • Respect DND (Do Not Disturb) registrations to avoid penalties

🔧 Troubleshooting

❌ Calls Not Starting

  • Check if KYC is fully approved
  • Verify telephony credentials are correctly entered in Settings → Telephony
  • Ensure your plan is active and not expired
  • Check if you've exceeded your concurrent call limit

🤖 AI Not Pitching Correct Product

  • Verify "Active Product to Pitch" matches a product name exactly (case-sensitive)
  • Check that the product details are saved and not empty
  • Review the system prompt — ensure it references the assigned product

📉 Low Answer Rates

  • Call during business hours (9 AM – 6 PM)
  • Avoid Mondays (people are catching up) and Fridays (people are winding down)
  • Ensure phone numbers include correct country codes
  • Retry no-answer leads up to 2 times with 24-hour gaps

💰 Unexpected Charges

  • Check Call Logs for abnormally long calls — AI may be stuck in loops
  • Review your prompt — overly verbose prompts increase TTS costs
  • Monitor your CPS usage; higher CPS plans cost more
